🌏 Landmarks — find these first
A0 La0
Lowest note on a standard piano — far left white key
C4 Do4
Middle C — centre of keyboard, just left of 2-black-key group
A4 La4
Concert A (440 Hz) — 6 white keys right of Middle C
C8 Do8
Highest note — the last white key on the far right
🔍 How to find any note
1 Look for groups of 2 black keys and 3 black keys alternating across the keyboard
2 C is always the white key just to the left of every 2-black-key group
3 Count up from C: C D E F G A B then C again (next octave)
4 The number (e.g. C4) tells you which octave — higher number = higher on the piano
